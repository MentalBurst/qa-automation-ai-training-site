🟣 Atomic Reproduction Steps

🟢 Scenario A Live Search with Debounce

  1. Assert result-count shows "20 books found"
  2. Type "ga" into search-input
  3. Assert search-debounce-indicator appears briefly
  4. Wait 400ms for debounce to complete
  5. Assert results are filtered (fewer than 20 cards visible)
  6. Assert all visible cards contain "ga" in title or author

🟢 Scenario B Empty State

  1. Type "zzzzzzzzz" into search-input
  2. Assert results-grid has no visible children
  3. Assert empty-state is visible
  4. Assert result-count shows "0 books found"
  5. Click btn-empty-clear
  6. Assert search input is cleared
  7. Assert result-count shows "20 books found"
