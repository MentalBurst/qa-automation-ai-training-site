🟣 Atomic Reproduction Steps

🟢 Scenario A Successful Registration

  1. Navigate to pages/02-registration-form.html
  2. Fill input-first-name with "Alice"
  3. Fill input-last-name with "Smith"
  4. Fill input-email with "alice@example.com"
  5. Fill input-username with "alice_smith"
  6. Fill input-password with "Secure@123"
  7. Assert password strength label shows "Strong" or "Very Strong"
  8. Fill input-confirm-password with "Secure@123"
  9. Select "Portugal" from select-country
  10. Check checkbox-terms
  11. Click btn-submit
  12. Assert reg-success is visible
  13. Assert reg-success-name contains "alice_smith"

🔴 Scenario B Password Mismatch

  1. Fill required fields with valid data
  2. Fill input-password with "Password1!"
  3. Fill input-confirm-password with "Different1!"
  4. Click btn-submit
  5. Assert error-confirm-password is visible
  6. Assert error text contains "match"

🟡 Pitfalls

  • Password strength meter updates on every keystroke test after full word typed.
  • Phone field is optional empty value must NOT trigger error.
  • Reset button clears form but does NOT navigate away.
